Concept — for engineer sign-off
Before anything else: prop the beam this week. The beam is visibly deflected and cracking under load, and more weight is being added above it. Install props under it now (Step 01 below), stop all pours and all brickwork that load this beam, and only then plan the permanent repair at normal speed. The propping removes the danger; the jacket removes the problem.
S-1

What is wrong with this beam

The garage front spans about 9 m clear. The beam over it is the standard 200 × 350 floor beam from the structural drawings — a section designed for the grid's spans of up to ≈ 4.2 m. At more than double its design span, it fails on stiffness (hence the visible bend) and is working at its strength limit (hence the cracking). The cracks are not a concrete-quality defect — they are the beam telling you it is overloaded.

Approximate bending moments. The demand figure assumes solid clay brick walls (Section S-5) and must be recomputed and signed by the engineer of record — but the ratio is the point: the beam has roughly a quarter of the capacity the span asks of it.

One question that changes the numbers — ask TIPSTEP in writing: "Do the first- and second-floor front columns land on this beam?" The drawings show grid columns at every level; if the intermediate columns were omitted only at ground level but built above, they are floating columns standing on this beam — concentrated point loads at midspan, which would explain the severity of the cracking. The jacket concept stays the same, but the calculation must use the real point loads (likely heavier bottom steel, e.g. 5–6 T20).

S-2

The fix: a reinforced-concrete jacket

New concrete and new reinforcement are cast around the existing beam, turning the 200 × 350 into a flat, uniform 300 × 700–750 beam correctly sized for 9 m. The flat shape was chosen deliberately over curved (fish-belly or arched) alternatives: straight formwork is the detail with the fewest ways to go wrong on a safety-critical repair, and the clean straight line suits the house's modern architecture. If an arched look is ever wanted over the parking, it gets added later as a cosmetic, non-structural fascia under this beam — never by shaping the structure itself. Headroom stays generous: ≈ 2.75 m clear under the full length of the beam.

S-3

The sequence, step by step

Order matters more than speed here. Steps 01–03 happen now; the rest at normal pace once the design is signed.

today

Prop and relieve

Props at ≤ 1.5 m centres per Fig. 5, snugged then tightened to lift the beam 3–5 mm. Props bear on sole plates on firm ground — never loose fill.

today

Freeze all loading above

No concrete pours and no brickwork on any bay that loads this beam until the jacket is cast and cured. Tell the masons explicitly — Section S-5.

this week

Mark and monitor the cracks

Pencil-mark each crack tip with the date; measure widths. Growth past a mark, or any new diagonal crack near a column, gets reported the same day.

week 1–2

Engineer's signed design

TIPSTEP produces the jacket drawing and calculation. Check it against the target table in S-2 — especially end anchorage, the floating-column question, and the end-column and footing checks.

week 2

Surface preparation

Hack off loose cover on soffit and sides, roughen to ~5 mm amplitude, expose the existing stirrups, blow clean, and saturate the surface before casting day.

week 2

Dowels and new cage

Drill and resin-set T12 dowels @ 250 mm into the old beam. Fix the new cage: 4 T20 bottom, side hangers, closed T8 links @ 150 (100 at ends). Spacers for 35 mm cover everywhere.

week 2

Column jackets and end anchorage

Jacket both end columns full height (footing to beam soffit, ≈ 350×350, 4T16 added verticals, T8 links, dowelled) — the top of each jacket is the collar the new beam bars anchor into per Figs. 4 & 6. This step is the difference between a beam and a decoration, and it costs only a day or two extra while the crew and formwork are already there.

week 3

Formwork and casting

Straight, tight forms — the flat soffit keeps this the simplest carpentry on site. Cast C25/30, 10 mm aggregate, plasticised, poured through the top via cored holes or a letterbox chute, vibrated well. Top up the final contact with the old soffit using a slightly expansive grout.

weeks 3–4

Cure wet for 7 days

Soaked hessian or polythene-wrapped, kept wet — same rule as every pour on this site, and doubly important on a structural repair.

week 7

Release at 28 days, then watch

Ease the props off slowly, re-inspect, repaint crack marks, and check monthly for 3 months. Marks that stay still = repair working.

S-4

The detail that decides everything: the end columns

Two things happen at each end of the beam, and both are load-bearing. First, anchorage: a bottom bar resists bending only if its ends are gripped — the new T20s must be anchored into the supports, not stopped at the column face. Second, the columns themselves: each 200 × 200 column now collects ≈ 140 kN of eccentric reaction it was never designed for, plus its existing loads from the floors above. The default plan solves both at once: jacket each column full height to ≈ 350 × 350 (4T16 added verticals, T8 links, dowelled to the existing column, footing to beam soffit) — the top of the jacket is the anchorage collar. The columns stay 200 × 200 only if TIPSTEP's calculation proves them adequate under the new reaction and its eccentricity, and the footings must be checked either way (≈ +70 kPa on the 1200 × 1200 pads).

S-5

Masonry: solid clay brick, 100 × 200 × 100

The walls are being built in solid fired clay brick, 10 × 20 × 10 cm — not hollow block. Structurally that is a fine material: durable, strong in compression, and as tied infill it stiffens the frame. But it is heavy, and every beam that carries a brick wall — this 9 m beam above all — must be designed for the real weight, not for hollow-block assumptions.

Wall build-up (200 mm + plaster)Weight per m² of wallLine load, 3.0 m wall
Solid clay brick (your spec)≈ 4.4 kN/m²≈ 13 kN/m
Hollow clay block (comparison)≈ 2.6 kN/m²≈ 8 kN/m

Roughly 70 % heavier than hollow block. The jacket targets in S-2 and the demand bar in S-1 already assume solid brick — make sure TIPSTEP's calculation does too.

Rule one for the masons: no brickwork on the 9 m beam, or on the slab bays it carries, until the jacket is cast and 28 days old. A 3 m solid-brick wall would add ≈ 13 kN per metre to a beam already past its limit.

Laying rules for this brick

  • Wet the bricks before laying. Dry fired clay sucks the water out of the mortar and the joint never gains strength. Dip or hose them ~10 minutes before use.
  • Mortar 1 : 4 (cement : sand), joints 10–12 mm, full and even — no hollow "furrowed" bed joints.
  • Bond the two wythes. A 200 mm wall from 100 mm bricks needs header courses (or ties) linking the two skins — English/Flemish bond, not two independent skins.
  • Hoop iron in walls under 200 mm at every alternate course — this is note 5 on your own drawings; hold the crew to it.
  • Tie panels to the columns — hoop iron or bent bars into the columns every 4th course, so infill and frame act together (this is what earns the seismic benefit).
  • Maximum lift ≈ 1.2 m of height per day so fresh mortar below isn't crushed.
  • Stop under beams with a snug final joint — pack the last gap tight (mortar + slate/brick slips) a day later, so the wall actually meets the structure.
S-6

Before you pay for the repair — the sign-off list

Take this list to TIPSTEP. Every box gets a yes, on paper, before casting day.

  • Jacket drawing shows a flat uniform section ≥ 300 × 650, bottom steel ≥ 4 T20 equivalent, and link spacing with the tighter zone at the ends.
  • Designed as a flat beam — bars carry all tension. Any arched look is a cosmetic, non-structural fascia added after the repair, never a reshaping of the structural section.
  • End anchorage detail is drawn explicitly (column-jacket collar or full-development resin anchors) — not left to the foreman.
  • The floating-column question answered in writing: do upper-floor front columns land on this beam? If yes, their point loads appear in the calculation.
  • End columns: full-height ≈ 350×350 jackets drawn, or a calculation proving the bare 200×200 columns adequate under the eccentric ≈ 140 kN reactions.
  • Footings at both end columns checked for the added ≈ 100 kN service load (≈ +70 kPa bearing); enlarged if the check fails.
  • Wall loads in the calculation use solid clay brick at ≈ 4.4 kN/m², not hollow block.
  • Method statement includes propping + 3–5 mm lift before casting, and props kept 28 days after.
  • Concrete: C25/30, 10 mm aggregate, plasticiser — and cubes taken on casting day.
  • Wet curing 7 days is someone's named responsibility.
  • Crack marks re-inspected at 1 and 3 months after prop release.

Cost frame: materials are ~1 m³ concrete, ~150 kg rebar, resin anchors and formwork — in the region of 1–1.5 M RWF including labour. If a quote lands far above that, ask for the breakdown; if far below, ask what was left out.

Mbere ya byose: shyira etayi (ibikingi) munsi y'umutambiko muri iki cyumweru. Umutambiko uragoramye kandi urasatagurika kubera uburemere burenze, kandi hejuru yawo hakomeza kongerwaho ibindi biremereye. Shyiramo etayi nonaha (Intambwe 01 hepfo), hagarika gusuka beto no kubaka amatafari ku bishingiye kuri uyu mutambiko, hanyuma gusana nyako gukorwe ku muvuduko usanzwe. Etayi ikuraho akaga; ikoti rikuraho ikibazo.
S-1

Ikibazo cy'uyu mutambiko

Imbere ya garage hari umwanya ufunguye wa metero 9. Umutambiko uwutwikiriye ni uwa 200 × 350 usanzwe mu bishushanyo — wagenewe intera zitarenze metero 4.2. Ku ntera irenze inshuro ebyiri iyo wagenewe, ntabasha guhagarara: ni yo mpamvu ugoramye (stiffness) kandi ukageza ku mbibi z'imbaraga zawo (strength). Imyenge (fissures) si ikimenyetso cya beto mbi — ni umutambiko ukubwira ko uremerewe.

Umwenge, 18 Nyakanga. Umwenge uca mu mutambiko wose uhagaze — ikimenyetso gisanzwe cy'uko uremerewe mu kugoragurika (flexion). Ukugorama. Urebye ku nsi y'umutambiko ubona uko wamanutse hagati burundu. Intera. Imbere ya garage ifunguye umutambiko utwaye — izagumaho nta nkingi hagati nk'uko nyir'inzu abyifuza.
Igishushanyo 1 — uko bigenda. Uburemere bw'amagorofa atatu n'inkuta ziri hejuru bugoramya intera ndende; imyenge ihagaze hagati yemeza ko ari ukugoragurika. Nihagaragara imyenge iberamye (diagonale) hafi y'inkingi, bimenyeshwe ako kanya — icyo cyitwa "shear" kandi kirakaze kurushaho.

Imibare igereranyije. Ikoresha uburemere bw'itafari ryuzuye (Igice S-5) kandi injeniyeri agomba kongera kuyibara no gushyiraho umukono — ariko icy'ingenzi ni iki: umutambiko ufite hafi kimwe cya kane cy'imbaraga intera imusaba.

Ikibazo kimwe gihindura imibare — baza TIPSTEP mu nyandiko: "Ese inkingi zo ku magorofa ya mbere n'iya kabiri zihagaze kuri uyu mutambiko?" Ibishushanyo byerekana inkingi kuri buri gorofa; niba hasi zaravanyweho ariko hejuru zarubatswe, izo ni inkingi zihagaze ku mutambiko ("floating columns") — uburemere bwibanze hagati, bisobanura n'impamvu imyenge ikabije. Igitekerezo cy'ikoti ntigihinduka, ariko kubara bigomba gukoresha uburemere nyakuri (birashoboka ko ibyuma byo hasi byiyongera, urugero 5–6 T20).

S-2

Igisubizo: ikoti rya beto (jacket)

Beto nshya n'ibyuma bishya bisukwa bikikije umutambiko uriho: uva kuri 200 × 350 ukaba umutambiko utambitse, uringaniye wa 300 × 700–750 ukwiranye na metero 9. Uburyo butambitse (flat) bwatoranyijwe ku bushake: kofraje igororotse ni yo ifite amakosa make ku murimo w'umutekano, kandi umurongo ugororotse ujyana n'imiterere igezweho y'inzu. Niba isura y'umuheto (arch) izifuzwa, izongerwaho nyuma nk'umutako utari uw'imbaraga munsi y'umutambiko — ntihazagira uhindura inyubako ubwayo. Munsi y'umutambiko hazasigara hafi metero 2.75 z'ubwisanzure ku burebure bwose.

Igishushanyo 2 — ikoti mu buryo burambuye (elevation). 300 × 700–750 kimwe ku burebure bwose; ibyuma bishya binyura ku buryo bugororotse bikinjizwa muri kola ku nkingi zombi. Isura y'umuheto, niba izifuzwa, izaba umutako woroheje gusa uzamanikwa munsi — ntushobora na rimwe gusimbura cyangwa kugabanya inyubako y'imbaraga.
Igishushanyo 3 — mbere na nyuma (coupe). Ahatavye igicucu ni beto nshya. Coupe ni imwe ku burebure bwose; ku nkingi igera mu makoti yazo (reba Ibishushanyo 4 na 6). Ibyuma bishya byo hasi biri ku burebure bwa ~700 mm — ubwo burebure, atari beto yiyongereye, ni bwo bwikuba gatanu imbaraga.
Igishushanyo 4 — plan ku nkingi. Umutambiko w'ikoti ugera ku nkingi ya 200 × 200; ibyuma byawo byinjira mu gace kunganirwa kayikikije, bikamanuka gufatira. Muri gahunda isanzwe ako gace ni hejuru y'ikoti ry'inkingi ryuzuye rya ~350 × 350 (Igishushanyo 6), rinakomeza inkingi ku buremere bushya bwa ~140 kN. Utumenyetso ku mpera z'ibyuma twerekana aho bimanukira.

S-3

Intambwe ku yindi

Uburyo burakurikirana — gukurikiza urutonde biruta kwihuta. Intambwe 01–03 zikorwa nonaha; izindi zikurikira igishushanyo kimaze gusinywa.

Igishushanyo 5 — Intambwe 01, gushyiraho etayi. Kuzikaza kugeza umutambiko uzamutse mm 3–5 byimurira uburemere muri etayi — ni ngombwa, kugira ngo ikoti risukwe ku mutambiko udaheze, kandi rizatware uburemere nyaryo rimaze gukomera.
uyu munsi

Shyiraho etayi, woroshye uburemere

Etayi ku ntera itarenze m 1.5 (Igishushanyo 5), zikazwa kugeza umutambiko uzamutse mm 3–5. Zishingira ku mbaho ku butaka bukomeye — ntabwo ari ku butaka bwasutswe.

uyu munsi

Hagarika uburemere bwose hejuru

Nta beto isukwa kandi nta tafari ryubakwa ahantu hose hashingiye kuri uyu mutambiko kugeza ikoti risutswe rikanakomera. Bibwire abubatsi b'amatafari ku mugaragaro — Igice S-5.

iki cyumweru

Shyira utumenyetso ku myenge, ukurikirane

Andikisha ikaramu itariki ku mpera ya buri mwenge; pima ubugari. Umwenge urenze akamenyetso, cyangwa mushya uberamye hafi y'inkingi — bimenyeshwa uwo munsi.

icyumweru 1–2

Igishushanyo cyemejwe n'injeniyeri

TIPSTEP atanga igishushanyo n'imibare by'ikoti. Bigereranywe n'imbonerahamwe S-2 — cyane cyane gufatira ku mpera, ikibazo cy'inkingi zihagaze ku mutambiko, n'igenzura ry'inkingi n'imisingi.

icyumweru 2

Gutegura ubuso

Gukuraho beto yoroshye ku nsi no ku mpande, kubagira ubuso (~5 mm), kugaragaza ama-etriye ariho, guhuha umukungugu no kuvomerera mbere y'umunsi wo gusuka.

icyumweru 2

Utubyuma duhuza n'ibyuma bishya

Gucukura no gushyiramo T12 buri 250 mm na resine. Gushyiraho ibyuma bishya: 4 T20 hasi, ibifata ku mpande, ama-etriye T8 buri 150 (100 ku mpera). Utwegereza (spacers) ku gipfuko cya 35 mm hose.

icyumweru 2

Amakoti y'inkingi no gufatira

Inkingi zombi zubakirwa ikoti ryuzuye (kuva ku musingi kugera munsi y'umutambiko, ~350×350, 4T16, T8, dowels) — hejuru y'iryo koti ni ho ibyuma by'umutambiko byinjira (Ibishushanyo 4 na 6). Iyi ntambwe ni yo itandukanya umutambiko n'umutako, kandi yongera iminsi 1–2 gusa kuri buri nkingi.

icyumweru 3

Kofraje no gusuka

Kofraje igororotse kandi ikomeye — uburyo butambitse ni bwo bworoshye ku babaji. Gusuka C25/30, kaburimbo 10 mm, plastifiant, binyuze mu myenge yacishijwe muri dari cyangwa mu kanwa ka kofraje, bigakubitwa na vibreur neza. Aho bihurira n'umutambiko wa kera hujujwe na mortier yaguka gato.

icyumweru 3–4

Kuvomerera iminsi 7

Ibigunira bitose cyangwa plastiki, bigumye bitose — nk'uko bisabwa kuri buri gusuka kuri uru rubuga, ariko hano ni itegeko ridakuka.

icyumweru 7

Gukuraho etayi ku minsi 28, hanyuma gukurikirana

Etayi zikurwaho buhoro buhoro, hongera hagasuzumwa, utumenyetso tw'imyenge tugasubirwamo, bigakurikiranwa buri kwezi mu mezi 3. Utumenyetso tudahinduka = gusana byakunze.

S-4

Ingingo ikomeye kurusha izindi: inkingi z'impera

Ku mpera z'umutambiko habera ibintu bibiri, byombi bitwara uburemere. Icya mbere: gufatira — icyuma cyo hasi kirwanya kugoragurika ari uko impera zacyo zifashwe; T20 nshya zigomba kwinjizwa mu nkingi, ntizihagarare ku ruhande rwazo. Icya kabiri: inkingi ubwazo — buri nkingi ya 200 × 200 yakira ~140 kN itari yagenewe, hiyongereyeho ibyo isanzwe itwaye biva ku magorofa yo hejuru. Gahunda isanzwe ikemura byombi icyarimwe: ikoti ryuzuye ry'inkingi rya ~350 × 350 (4T16 nshya, ama-etriye T8, dowels, kuva ku musingi kugera munsi y'umutambiko) — hejuru y'ikoti ni yo kola ibyuma byinjiramo. Inkingi zigumaho 200 × 200 gusa ari uko imibare ya TIPSTEP ibyemeje, kandi imisingi igenzurwa uko byagenda kose (~+70 kPa kuri pads za 1200 × 1200).

Igishushanyo 6 — inkingi y'impera. Ikoti riva ku musingi rikagera munsi y'umutambiko; igice cyo hejuru ni cyo ibyuma by'umutambiko byinjiramo bikamanuka (nk'uko bigaragara muri plan y'Igishushanyo 4). Ubundi buryo — resine gusa mu nkingi isanzwe — bwemewe gusa ari uko injeniyeri yerekanye uburebure buhagije bwo gufatira kandi akemeza ko inkingi ya 200×200 ihagije. Babaze icyo bahisemo, mu nyandiko.
S-5

Ariko biraremereye — buri mutambiko utwaye urukuta rw'amatafari, cyane cyane uyu wa metero 9, ugomba kubarwa hakoreshejwe uburemere nyakuri, atari ubwa blocs.

Amabwiriza yo kubaka iri tafari

  • Kuvomerera amatafari mbere yo kubaka. Itafari ryumye rinyunyuza amazi ya mortier, isura ntikomere. Kuyakoza mu mazi iminota ~10 mbere yo kuyubaka.
  • Mortier 1 : 4 (sima : umusenyi), isura ya mm 10–12, yuzuye kandi iringaniye — nta sura zirimo ubusa.
  • Guhuza ibice byombi by'urukuta. Urukuta rwa 200 mm rubakishijwe amatafari ya 100 mm rukeneye imirongo y'amatafari atambitse (headers) cyangwa utubyuma duhuza ibice byombi — ntabwo ari ibice bibiri byigenga.
  • Hoop iron (agapira) buri murongo w'inyuma ku nkuta ziri munsi ya 200 mm — ni icyanditswe ku bishushanyo byanyu (note 5); bikurikizwe.
  • Guhuza inkuta n'inkingi — hoop iron cyangwa utubyuma twinjizwa mu nkingi buri mirongo 4, kugira ngo urukuta n'inkingi bikorane (ni byo bizana n'akamaro ku mutingito).
  • Ntihubakwe hejuru ya m 1.2 z'uburebure ku munsi umwe, kugira ngo mortier yo hasi idahetwa itarakomera.
  • Guhagarara munsi y'imitambiko n'isura ifunganye — umwanya wa nyuma wuzuzwa ku munsi ukurikira (mortier + uduce tw'itafari), urukuta rugahura n'inyubako koko.
S-6

Mbere yo kwishyura — urutonde rwo kugenzura

Jyana uru rutonde kwa TIPSTEP. Buri kintu kibona "yego" mu nyandiko, mbere y'umunsi wo gusuka.

  • Igishushanyo cy'ikoti cyerekana ingano ihoraho ≥ 300 × 650, ibyuma byo hasi ≥ 4 T20 (cyangwa bingana na byo), n'ama-etriye yegeranye ku mpera.
  • Bubatswe nk'umutambiko utambitse — isura y'umuheto izaba umutako utari uw'imbaraga, wongerwaho nyuma, ntihagire uhindura inyubako ubwayo.
  • Gufatira ku mpera bishushanyijwe ku mugaragaro (kola y'ikoti ry'inkingi cyangwa resine ifite uburebure buhagije) — ntibisigirwa kapita.
  • Ikibazo cy'inkingi zihagaze ku mutambiko gisubijwe mu nyandiko: ese inkingi zo hejuru zihagaze kuri uyu mutambiko? Niba ari yego, uburemere bwazo buri mu kubara.
  • Inkingi z'impera: amakoti yuzuye ya ~350×350 ashushanyijwe, cyangwa imibare yemeza ko inkingi za 200×200 zihagije ku burinzi bwa ~140 kN butaringaniye.
  • Imisingi y'inkingi zombi yagenzuwe ku ~+100 kN yiyongereye (~+70 kPa); yagurwa igenzura ryanze.
  • Uburemere bw'inkuta mu kubara ni ubw'itafari ryuzuye (~4.4 kN/m²), atari ubwa blocs.
  • Uburyo bw'imirimo burimo etayi + kuzamura mm 3–5 mbere yo gusuka, na etayi igumaho iminsi 28 nyuma yo gusuka.
  • Beto: C25/30, kaburimbo 10 mm, plastifiant — na cubes z'igerageza zifatwa umunsi wo gusuka.
  • Kuvomerera iminsi 7 bifite umuntu ubishinzwe w'izina rizwi; imyenge isuzumwa nyuma y'ukwezi 1 n'amezi 3 etayi zivuyeho.

Ikigereranyo cy'ikiguzi: beto ~1 m³, ibyuma ~150 kg, resine na kofraje — hafi 1–1.5 M RWF harimo n'umurimo, hiyongereyeho iminsi 1–2 kuri buri nkingi ku makoti yazo. Igiciro kiri hejuru cyane: saba ibisobanuro birambuye; kiri hasi cyane: baza icyasizwe hanze.

Byateguwe ku wa 20 Kanama 2026 hashingiwe ku mafoto y'urubuga no ku bishushanyo bya TIPSTEP (Plan No. 8045). Iyi nyandiko ni igitekerezo n'urwego rwo kugenzura ubuziranenge ku nyir'inzu — si igishushanyo cyo kubaka. Ingano, ibyuma n'ibisobanuro bya nyuma bigomba gushushanywa, kugenzurwa no gusinywa n'injeniyeri ubifite mu nshingano mbere y'uko imirimo ikomeza. Ibipimo byose ni muri milimetero keretse aho byanditswe ukundi.
